The MW4 Beta Is Live. Here's What Happens to This Database.

Modern Warfare 4's beta opened on 21 August. What that means for 51 pages of Black Ops 7 settings, and why no MW4 numbers are going up yet.

The Modern Warfare 4 beta went live on Friday, straight out of the Call of Duty: NEXT broadcast. Weekend One ran 21 to 25 August for pre-orders and code holders. The open beta — everyone, every platform, Switch 2 included — runs 28 August to 1 September, and the game itself lands on 23 October.

Which raises the obvious question for a site that is 51 pages of Black Ops 7 settings: what now.

The short version is that nothing here gets deleted and nothing here gets relabelled as MW4. Every tracked player page, every team page and the whole stats layer stays up as a record of the Black Ops 7 season, which is the last season those numbers describe. BO7's competitive life ended at EWC Paris and it is not coming back, but a settings archive for a finished season is still worth having — people were reading pro Modern Warfare III settings well into the following year.

What is not happening is the thing several settings sites will do this month, which is to swap the game name at the top of the page and leave the numbers underneath. A sensitivity figure is meaningful against one game's turn rate and stick scaling and nothing else. Treyarch and Infinity Ward have not used the same deadzone scale, so a 3 / 99 right stick does not have an obvious MW4 equivalent yet. MW4 also ships a reworked movement system with a training course attached to it, and movement is what sensitivity is tuned around. Republishing BO7 values under an MW4 heading would be inventing data, and the point of this site is that it does not do that.

So the plan is the boring one. Pros stream the beta with their menus open, the same way they did all through BO7, and those captures go up on a new MW4 page as they happen — marked as beta values, because a beta menu is a snapshot and settings will move again between weekends and at launch. Verified season data starts once the 2027 rosters are locked and practice is happening on the retail build. Same verification standard, same requirement of a source and a capture date, same blank field where neither exists.

One thing does carry over in the meantime, and it is worth saying plainly because it is the part people actually want. The exact BO7 numbers will not transfer, but the reasoning behind them will. A wide FOV, a right stick minimum just above zero rather than at it, a left stick maximum short of the top of the range, a sensitivity slower than most ranked players assume — those are choices about how a controller behaves under pressure, not about which Call of Duty is installed. Those are on the MW4 page with the league figures behind them, and in more depth in the guides.

The 2027 season will be played on this game. The database starts filling up again from here.
