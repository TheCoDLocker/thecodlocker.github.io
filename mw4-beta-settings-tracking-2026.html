Tracking Pro Settings in the MW4 Beta

BO7 setups give us a starting reference for MW4. Here’s what those records can tell you and what still needs a beta source.

A new CoD doesn’t mean you have to rebuild your controller setup on day one. Starting with familiar settings lets you learn the maps and movement without changing everything under your thumb at the same time.

That’s why the BO7 records are useful during the MW4 beta. They give you a reference for each player. Until there’s a source showing that setup in MW4, though, the two shouldn’t be treated as the same record.

The BO7 numbers we have to work from

Of the 45 tracked BO7 sensitivities, 21 are at 1.70. The average is 1.74 and the range is 1.60–2.10. FOV averages 101.2, with 18 players at 100. You’ll also see the left 1/75 and right 3/99 deadzone combination often.

Those are BO7 database figures, not a claim that all 45 players have shown the same values in the beta. The MW4 page is the place to check the available beta information and its sources.

What needs a fresh check?

Weapon classes need their own MW4 records, and graphics options need to be checked in the new menu. Even settings with familiar names can feel different after changes to movement, weapons or aim assist.

A beta class also isn’t a settled competitive class. Builds may change with patches, and the eventual league rules can narrow the options further.

What to use in the meantime

If your existing setup feels comfortable, start there. Make a change when you can identify something specific that isn’t working, rather than because a new game has arrived.

Recorded changes are collected on the updates page. Check the date and game attached to a setting before copying it.
