Pros Didn't Change Their Settings for MW4

The MW4 beta is live and the league went in on its Black Ops 7 numbers. Sens and FOV for 45 CDL pros, usable in the open beta right now.

The Modern Warfare 4 beta went live on Friday, straight out of the Call of Duty: NEXT broadcast. Weekend One ran 21 to 25 August for pre-orders and code holders, the open beta runs 28 August to 1 September on every platform including Switch 2, and the game itself lands on 23 October.

The interesting part, for a site that is 51 pages of controller settings, is how little happened. Pros went into the beta on the setups they finished the Black Ops 7 season with. Sensitivity and field of view are unchanged almost across the board.

This gets treated as surprising every time a new Call of Duty lands, and it should not be. Sensitivity is not a preference a pro re-tunes each October — it is years of muscle memory attached to a specific number, and several players in the database have carried theirs through three or four releases. Beta weekend, with unfamiliar maps and a movement system nobody has learned yet, is the worst possible time to start over on your stick. If anything the incentive runs the other way: keep the one thing that is constant and let the map knowledge catch up.

Which means the settings on this site are usable right now rather than in eight weeks. 21 of the 45 tracked pros run 1.70 sensitivity, the league average is 1.74, and the whole professional field fits between 1.60 and 2.10 — a far narrower band than any ranked lobby. On FOV, 18 sit at 100 with an average of 101.2. Deadzones cluster hard too: a right stick minimum of 3 rather than 0, and a left stick maximum of 75 to reach full movement speed with less travel. The full table is on the MW4 settings page, filterable by player or team.

What genuinely cannot carry over is everything hanging off the weapons. Competitive classes are gone until the league publishes a 2027 ruleset, because a class list without a GA list is just a loadout. ADS multipliers by zoom only mean something against optics that ship, and MW4 rebuilds Gunsmith with an attachment tier unlocked by levelling a gun. Graphics and audio settings sit in a menu that is not final. None of that is going up as MW4 data on the strength of a beta build.

The full update comes at launch on 23 October: every player re-captured against the retail game, capture dates reset, classes rebuilt once there is a ruleset to build them against. Until then the numbers above are what the league is playing on, and anyone who moves off theirs gets flagged on the updates page as it happens.
