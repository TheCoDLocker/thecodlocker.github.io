Answers

Frequently asked

Answers taken straight off the values tracked above.

Does Alluka play on a high sensitivity?

A little above average. Alluka runs 1.75 on both sticks where the league average is 1.74.

Does Alluka play on a wide FOV?

About average, really. 102 against a league mean of 101.

Why is Alluka's right stick deadzone unusual?

1/60 on the left and 1/99 on the right. The right stick minimum of 1 is lower than almost anyone else in the league runs. That's the aggressive end of the trade: the finest aim control on offer, with a little drift accepted as the price rather than tuned out.

What controller does Alluka use?

A Battle Beaver custom (PS5), no paddles.

Is Alluka faster or slower than the rest of Riyadh Falcons?

Slower, on 1.75 against 1.81 for the rest of the roster.
