Answers

Frequently asked

The questions people actually ask about this setup.

What sensitivity is aBeZy on?

1.65 on both sticks, under the 1.74 league average. Lower numbers prioritise small adjustments at range, where too much stick speed makes shots hard to hold.

Does aBeZy play on a wide FOV?

About average, really. 100 against a league mean of 101.

Can you really play on a zero deadzone?

aBeZy does, on the left stick, running 0/70 left and 3/99 right. It only holds up on sticks with no drift at rest. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does aBeZy use?

A PS4 pad with a Marius board. The builder isn't confirmed.

Does aBeZy change the default layout?

aBeZy runs a stick and move + tactical button layout, so it's worth checking that before you copy the rest of the sheet across.
