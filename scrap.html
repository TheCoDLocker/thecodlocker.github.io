Answers

Frequently asked

Common questions, answered from the tracked figures.

Does Scrap play on the most common CDL sensitivity?

Yes. Scrap runs 1.7 on both sticks, and more tracked players use that number than any other.

Why is Scrap's FOV so low?

At 98 it's the tightest setting tracked. Pulling FOV down makes enemies read larger on screen at range, which is the trade players holding long sightlines tend to take since most of their gunfights happen at distance.

What deadzones does Scrap run?

1/75 left and 3/99 right. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does Scrap use?

A Battle Beaver custom (PS4).

How does Scrap compare to the rest of Los Angeles Thieves?

Closely. 1.70 against a 1.68 teammate average, so the roster runs near-identical stick speeds.
