Answers

Frequently asked

The questions people actually ask about this setup.

What sensitivity does Neptune run?

1.7 horizontal and 1.75 vertical, which happens to be the busiest number in the league.

Where does Neptune's FOV sit in the league?

Mid-pack, on 102. The tracked field runs 98 to 107.

Does Neptune play claw?

Yes, one of nine tracked players on it. The index finger covers the face buttons so the thumb never has to leave the stick.

Can you really play on a zero deadzone?

Neptune does, on the left stick, running 0/75 left and 3/99 right. It only holds up on sticks with no drift at rest. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does Neptune use?

A PS4 pad with a Marius board. The builder isn't confirmed.
