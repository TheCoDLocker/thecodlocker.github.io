Answers

Frequently asked

What the numbers above work out to in practice.

What sensitivity does Fire run?

1.7 on both sticks, which happens to be the busiest number in the league.

Who runs the widest FOV in the CDL?

Fire does, on 107, against a league average of 101. Settings this wide suit close-quarters play, where seeing more of what's in front of you is worth more than picking out targets at distance. SMG players lean this way more often, though it's a tendency rather than a rule.

How are Fire's sticks set up?

1/75 and 3/99. Movement matters enough in this game that the left stick is tuned to hit full speed early, while the right is set for the finest aim control that won't introduce drift.

What controller does Fire use?

A PS5 controller (2 back buttons), which puts Fire in the minority. Most of the tracked field is on something custom.

Is Fire faster or slower than the rest of Carolina Royal Ravens?

Slower, on 1.70 against 1.92 for the rest of the roster.
