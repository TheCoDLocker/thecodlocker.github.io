Which Controllers Show Up Most in the CDL?

Battle Beaver is the most common recorded builder. Marius board entries are counted separately because they describe a different part of the controller.

Battle Beaver appears most often among the named builders in these records, with 18 of the 47 controller entries. There are also 22 entries mentioning a Marius board. Those counts describe different parts of a setup, so adding them together would be misleading.

A controller can be built by Battle Beaver and have a Marius board inside it. One label identifies the builder; the other identifies a component. Where a record only names the board, it doesn’t necessarily establish who built the whole controller.
