Answers

Frequently asked

Common questions, answered from the tracked figures.

Is Dashy slower than most CDL pros?

A bit. 1.65 on both sticks against a 1.74 average, with 96% of the field running faster.

Why is Dashy's FOV so low?

At 98 it's the tightest setting tracked. Pulling FOV down makes enemies read larger on screen at range, which is the trade players holding long sightlines tend to take since most of their gunfights happen at distance.

What deadzones does Dashy run?

1/75 left and 3/99 right. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What pad is Dashy on?

A Battle Beaver custom (PS4) running a Marius 5-pin board for native 8 kHz polling.

Is Dashy faster or slower than the rest of OpTic Texas?

Slower, on 1.65 against 1.70 for the rest of the roster.
