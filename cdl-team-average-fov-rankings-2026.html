Which CDL Team Has the Highest Average FOV?

All 12 CDL teams ranked by the average FOV of their tracked players, with sample sizes and team ranges.

Paris Gentle Mates run the widest average FOV in the league at 103.4. Boston Breach run the narrowest at 99.3. The gap between the two extremes of a twelve-team league is four degrees, which tells you most of what you need to know about how much of a real choice this setting is at the top level.

The league-wide player average is 101.2, and the full tracked range runs 98 to 107. But the distribution is not spread evenly across that range — it is a spike at 100 with a tail either side.

Forty-five tracked players with a confirmed FOV. No tracked player runs 104 or 106.

Eighteen of forty-five sit exactly on 100. The next largest groups are 102 and 103. At the edges, Sib and Fire both run 107, and Dashy, Scrap and Spart all run 98.

One thing worth testing, since it comes up constantly: does a faster player run a wider FOV? Across the 45 tracked players the correlation between sensitivity and FOV is 0.18 — weak enough to treat as noise. Players at or below 1.70 sensitivity average 100.9 FOV; players above 1.70 average 101.8. Under a degree apart. Whatever drives a player's FOV choice, it is not their stick speed.
