Contact

Corrections, settings updates and missing sources are all welcome. This site is maintained by one person, and most of what gets fixed here gets fixed because a reader sent something in.

Email

thecodlocker7@gmail.com

Sending a settings correction

The useful thing to include is the source, not just the number. In rough order of how quickly it can be applied:

• A clip or VOD timestamp where the player has their settings menu open
• A screenshot of the player's !settings command output, with the channel visible
• A broadcast segment showing the values
• A link to the player or team posting them directly

With one of those attached, the value is updated and the change is logged on the updates page with the new capture date. Without a source, it goes on a list to be confirmed rather than being published straight away — not because reports are unwelcome, but because an unsourced number is exactly what this site is trying not to be.

Everything else

Broken pages, wrong roster information, missing players, or anything about the site itself can go to the same address. Expect a reply within a few days rather than the same hour — this is run alongside other things.
