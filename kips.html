Answers

Frequently asked

Short answers on how this setup compares with the league.

Does Kips play on a high sensitivity?

A little above average. Kips runs 1.85 on both sticks where the league average is 1.74.

Does Kips use a wide FOV?

Wider than most. Kips runs 105 against a league average of 101. That end of the range leans toward players who fight close and want the peripheral view more than they need distant targets to read large.

Why is Kips's left stick maximum so low?

2/70 on the left, 2/99 on the right. The left maximum of 70 does the usual job: full movement input lands before the stick hits the edge, so top speed comes on sooner.

What controller does Kips use?

A PS5 pad with a Marius board. The builder isn't confirmed.

Is Kips faster or slower than the rest of Toronto KOI?

Faster, on 1.85 against 1.67 for the rest of the roster.
