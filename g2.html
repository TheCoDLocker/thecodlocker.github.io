Stick speed

All four tracked players run exactly 1.70. G2 is the only roster in the league with zero spread — every other team, including the tightest, has at least 0.05 between its fastest and slowest player. Whether that is coincidence or a team standard is not something the data settles.

Deadzones

Frequently asked

What sensitivity does G2 Minnesota use in Black Ops 7?

Every tracked player on the roster runs 1.7. That is below the 1.74 league average and makes G2 Minnesota the 8th fastest team of twelve.

Who runs the widest FOV on G2 Minnesota?

Skyz at 102. The tightest on the roster is Envoy at 100, and the team averages 101 against a 101.2 league average — 7th widest of twelve.

What deadzones does G2 Minnesota run?

2 of 4 tracked players run 1/75 left and 3/99 right. Kremp and Nastie sit off that profile — the per-player figures are in the table above.

Does anyone on G2 Minnesota play claw?

No claw grip has been confirmed for anyone on the roster. Grip is only recorded here when a player has shown or stated it, so an absence means unconfirmed rather than ruled out.

What controllers does G2 Minnesota use?

Pads on record: PS4 custom, builder unconfirmed (2), SCUF Impact (1) and PS5 custom, builder unconfirmed (1). Kremp, Nastie and Skyz run a Marius board for native 8 kHz polling.

Are all G2 Minnesota settings confirmed?

Yes — all 4 players on the roster have sourced settings. Each player page carries its own capture date and source.
