Answers

Frequently asked

The questions people actually ask about this setup.

What sensitivity is Abuzah on?

1.65 on both sticks, under the 1.74 league average. Lower numbers prioritise small adjustments at range, where too much stick speed makes shots hard to hold.

What FOV does Abuzah use in Black Ops 7?

100, which is about the league average of 101.

Does Abuzah play claw?

Yes, one of nine tracked players on it. The index finger covers the face buttons so the thumb never has to leave the stick.

Why is Abuzah's left stick maximum so low?

1/75 on the left, 3/99 on the right. The left maximum of 75 does the usual job: full movement input lands before the stick hits the edge, so top speed comes on sooner.

What pad is Abuzah on?

A Battle Beaver custom (PS4), Marius board.
