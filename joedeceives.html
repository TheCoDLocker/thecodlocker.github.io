Frequently asked

Quick answers, all of them from the data on this page.

Is JoeDeceives faster than the CDL average?

Slightly. 1.83 on both sticks against a 1.74 average, which is faster than 89% of the tracked field.

What FOV does JoeDeceives play on?

103, the wide end of a league that runs 98 to 107. More of the map on screen helps up close, where you're clearing tight angles and want to see what's in front of you.

How are JoeDeceives's sticks set up?

1/75 and 2/99. Movement matters enough in this game that the left stick is tuned to hit full speed early, while the right is set for the finest aim control that won't introduce drift.

What controller does JoeDeceives use?

A DualSense Edge (PS5), which puts JoeDeceives in the minority. Most of the tracked field is on something custom.

Is JoeDeceives faster or slower than the rest of Paris Gentle Mates?

Faster, on 1.83 against 1.74 for the rest of the roster.
