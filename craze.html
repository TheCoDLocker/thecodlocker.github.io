Frequently asked

The questions people actually ask about this setup.

How fast is Craze's sensitivity?

1.75 on both sticks, which puts Craze above 62% of tracked players against a 1.74 average. Quicker sticks help with turning and manoeuvring up close, which is the usual reason SMG players tend to sit above the mean.

Is Craze's field of view unusually tight?

Not by much. 99 is below average, but the tighter end of the league is where range-heavy players tend to settle.

What deadzones does Craze run?

1/75 left and 3/99 right. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does Craze use?

A Battle Beaver custom (PS5).

How does Craze compare to the rest of Vancouver Surge?

Closely. 1.75 against a 1.73 teammate average, so the roster runs near-identical stick speeds.
