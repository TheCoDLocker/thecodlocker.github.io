The Settings Behind FaZe Vegas’s Champs Win

Simp, Abuzah, 04 and Drazah won with setups close to the rest of the field. A look at the numbers recorded on their pages.

FaZe Vegas beat OpTic 5–2 in the 2026 Champs final. After a result like that, it’s natural to want a look at the winners’ setups. Their recorded controller settings are fairly close to what you’ll find elsewhere in the database.

What the four players use

Simp is listed at 1.70 horizontal and vertical sensitivity with 100 FOV. Abuzah uses 1.65 sensitivity. 04 is at 1.70 and 102 FOV, while Drazah is at 1.70 and 100.

The full player pages include deadzones, layouts and hardware. Those details matter if you want to understand the setup beyond the two numbers on a card.

What to take from the comparison

None of these sensitivities is unusual in the tracked field. That makes the setups useful references, but the match result doesn’t show that any particular setting caused the win.

If you want to try one, start with the player whose settings are closest to yours. Give it a few sessions and check whether it feels comfortable before changing anything else.
