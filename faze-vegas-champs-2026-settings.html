FaZe Vegas Won It All. Their Settings Are Ordinary.

The world champions play on numbers half the league uses. What that tells you about copying pros.

FaZe Vegas are your 2026 world champions — 5–2 over OpTic in the Vegas grand final, no three-peat. Simp took MVP for his third ring, first player to get three without all of them coming on OpTic. First rings for Abuzah and 04. Second for Drazah.

So what do the champions play on? Nothing unusual. Simp: 1.7 both sticks, 100 FOV, Marius. Abuzah: 1.65, claw. 04: 1.7 at 102. Drazah: 1.7 at 100 on a Battle Beaver. Those numbers sit within a few clicks of the team they beat on every axis.

People go looking for the secret in the settings. There isn't one. The gap between winning and losing that final lived somewhere settings can't reach. The champions running ordinary numbers is a point in favour of picking something inside the league's band and leaving it alone.

All four sheets are on their player pages, deadzones and hardware included.
