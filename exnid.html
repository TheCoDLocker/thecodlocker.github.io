Answers

Frequently asked

What the numbers above work out to in practice.

What sensitivity does Exnid run?

1.7 on both sticks, which happens to be the busiest number in the league.

How wide is Exnid's field of view?

103, and only a handful of tracked players go wider. The trade for the extra view is that enemies at range sit smaller on screen.

Can you really play on a zero deadzone?

Exnid does, on the left stick, running 0/70 left and 3/99 right. It only holds up on sticks with no drift at rest. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does Exnid use?

A PS4 pad with a Marius board. The builder isn't confirmed.

Is Exnid faster or slower than the rest of Riyadh Falcons?

Slower, on 1.70 against 1.82 for the rest of the roster.
