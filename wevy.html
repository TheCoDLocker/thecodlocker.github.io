Answers

Frequently asked

Common questions, answered from the tracked figures.

Is Wevy faster than the CDL average?

Slightly. 1.8 on both sticks against a 1.74 average, which is faster than 80% of the tracked field.

What FOV does Wevy play on?

105, the wide end of a league that runs 98 to 107. More of the map on screen helps up close, where you're clearing tight angles and want to see what's in front of you.

Why is Wevy's left stick maximum so low?

1/75 on the left, 2/99 on the right. The left maximum of 75 does the usual job: full movement input lands before the stick hits the edge, so top speed comes on sooner.

What controller does Wevy use?

A DualSense (PS5), default, which puts Wevy in the minority. Most of the tracked field is on something custom.

How does Wevy compare to the rest of Cloud9 New York?

Closely. 1.80 against a 1.77 teammate average, so the roster runs near-identical stick speeds.
