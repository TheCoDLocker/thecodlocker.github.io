MW4 Pro Settings

Controller settings for the MW4 season, read off the pros’ own menus, and what changed from Black Ops 7.

MW4 launched on October 23 and the 2027 CDL season starts December 4. Every player page now leads with MW4: new settings where they have been captured, last season’s numbers where they haven’t, and the 2026 Black Ops 7 record in an archive tab.

Nobody rebuilt their setup for the beta. Most of the league has been on the same sensitivity for years, so a new game with new maps and new movement is the last place they'd want to relearn their stick. They carried everything over and started playing.

Launch has moved a few numbers. Nine of the 15 pros with MW4 settings on record changed something from last season, listed below. Classes have their own page now: 17 pro builds with every attachment.
