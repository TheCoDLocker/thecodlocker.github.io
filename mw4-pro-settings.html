What the two have in common

The classes line up more closely than the settings do. Nine confirmed attachment slots across the two guns, and not one of them is an optic, a laser or a magazine. Both players spend everything on muzzle, barrel, grip and ammunition — recoil control, range and movement speed. Both run a flash hider and a ZLR barrel, and both run an angled grip.

The controller config matches too. Default Flipped, bumpers swapped onto the triggers, vibration off, bumper ping off. Sensitivity is 1.75 and 1.65, which is where both of them already were on Black Ops 7. Neither changed it for the beta.

Where they split is the left stick. Shotzzy caps his at 75, so the stick reaches maximum movement input about three-quarters of the way through its travel. Dashy runs 98, close to the full range. Both are common across the league. The low cap tends to turn up with players who want the sprint threshold to trigger sooner, and it is worth testing rather than copying — more on that in the deadzone guide.

Trigger deadzones split the same way: 0 and 10 for Shotzzy, 20 and 20 for Dashy. Very low trigger values usually point to hair-trigger hardware, where the trigger barely travels to begin with. On a stock pad those same numbers behave differently, so read that field as a hardware note rather than a preference.

Frequently asked

What class is Shotzzy running in the MW4 beta?

The ISO submachine gun, using the La Sombra Venenosa blueprint from the ISO Nightshade set. Five attachments: TA Lightning R3M flash hider, ZLR Carbon-900 long barrel, FSS TAC90 angled short grip, No Stock, and 9mm Overpressured ammunition.

What M4 class is Dashy running in the MW4 beta?

A TZL-110 V1 flash hider, the ZLR Drift 16.5-inch light barrel, the FSS Fireline angled grip and 5.56 NATO Frangible ammunition. The fifth attachment was covered by his stream overlay, so it is left blank here rather than guessed.

What sensitivity do CDL pros use in the MW4 beta?

The same one they used on Black Ops 7. 21 of the 45 tracked pros run 1.70, the average is 1.74, and the whole league sits between 1.60 and 2.10.

What FOV do pros use in MW4?

18 of 45 are on 100. The average is 101.2 and nobody tracked goes below 98 or above 107.

Did pros change their settings for MW4?

Not really. Almost everyone carried their Black Ops 7 setup over. Most pros have had the same sens for years and stick with what they know through a game change.

Will these settings be updated?

Yes. Anything that changes gets updated here as it happens, and there is a full pass at launch on 23 October when classes and the 2027 ruleset exist.

When does the MW4 beta run?

Weekend one was 21 to 25 August for pre-orders. The open beta runs 28 August to 1 September on all platforms, Switch 2 included.
