The thing everyone expected at a title change mostly did not happen. Pros did not rebuild their setups for Modern Warfare 4. The overwhelming majority went into the beta on the same numbers they finished the Black Ops 7 season with, and sensitivity and field of view in particular are unchanged almost across the board.

That is less surprising the longer you have watched it. A pro sensitivity is muscle memory measured in years rather than a per-title preference — several players on this list have run the same number through three or four Call of Duty releases. A new game is exactly the wrong moment to start relearning your stick. What does get rebuilt at a title change is everything downstream of the weapons: classes, attachments, per-optic ADS multipliers, and eventually whatever the league bans. The stick stays where it is.

So the table below is usable now, in the open beta, rather than in eight weeks.

What the league actually runs

  • Sensitivity — 1.70 is the standard. 21 of the 45 tracked pros are on it. The average is 1.74 and the whole league fits between 1.60 and 2.10, which is a much narrower band than most ranked lobbies.
  • FOV — 100 is the most common. 18 pros sit there, the average is 101.2, and nobody tracked goes below 98 or above 107.
  • Right stick minimum sits just above zero. 3 is the effective floor across the league rather than 0, because a true zero picks up stick drift on a pad that has seen a season of use.
  • Left stick maximum sits below the top of the range. 75 is the common value, which reaches full movement speed with less stick travel.

What still gets re-checked at launch

Sens, FOV, deadzones, layout and grip are stable and listed above. The fields that genuinely cannot carry over are the ones tied to content that does not exist yet:

  • Competitive classes. The 2027 ruleset has not been published, so there is no GA list to build against. Beta loadouts are not competitive classes and are not being posted as if they were.
  • ADS multipliers by zoom. These are only meaningful against the optics that ship, and MW4 rebuilds Gunsmith with an attachment tier unlocked by levelling a weapon.
  • Graphics and audio. New build, new menu, and the beta's options are not final.

What happens on 23 October

A full pass on the retail build. Every tracked player re-captured against MW4's own menus, capture dates reset, classes rebuilt once the league publishes a ruleset for 2027, and the six players still missing settings picked up if they surface. Anyone who does move off their Black Ops 7 numbers between now and then gets flagged on the updates page rather than changed silently, and everything follows the same verification standard as the rest of the site — a source and a date, or the field stays blank.

Frequently asked

What sensitivity are CDL pros using in Modern Warfare 4?

The same numbers they finished the Black Ops 7 season on, in almost every case. 21 of 45 tracked pros run 1.70, which makes it the league standard by a distance. The average is 1.74 and the full range is 1.60 to 2.10.

What FOV do CDL pros use in MW4?

18 of 45 sit at 100, the average is 101.2, and the range runs 98 to 107. Field of view is the setting pros change least often — most pick a number early in a career and leave it there across titles.

Did pros change their settings for Modern Warfare 4?

Mostly no. The great majority went into the beta on their existing setup, and sensitivity and FOV in particular have barely moved. What does get rebuilt at a title change is everything downstream of the weapons — classes, attachments and per-optic ADS multipliers.

Are these confirmed Modern Warfare 4 captures?

They are the settings pros are running in the beta, carried over from sourced Black Ops 7 captures. Every player page still shows the original source and capture date behind its values. When does the Modern Warfare 4 beta run?

Weekend One ran 21 to 25 August 2026 as early access for pre-orders and beta codes. Weekend Two is the open beta, 28 August to 1 September 2026, with no pre-order required and Nintendo Switch 2 included.

When is the full MW4 settings update?

At launch, 23 October 2026. Beta menus change between weekends and change again at retail, so the complete pass — every player re-captured, capture dates reset, classes rebuilt against the 2027 ruleset — happens on the shipping game rather than on a beta build.
