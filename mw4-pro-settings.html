Frequently asked

What sensitivity do CDL pros use in the MW4 beta?

The same one they used on Black Ops 7. 21 of the 45 tracked pros run 1.70, the average is 1.74, and the whole league sits between 1.60 and 2.10.

What FOV do pros use in MW4?

18 of 45 are on 100. The average is 101.2 and nobody tracked goes below 98 or above 107.

Did pros change their settings for MW4?

Not really. Almost everyone carried their Black Ops 7 setup over. Most pros have had the same sens for years and stick with what they know through a game change.

Will these settings be updated?

Yes. Anything that changes gets updated here as it happens, and there is a full pass at launch on 23 October when classes and the 2027 ruleset exist.

When does the MW4 beta run?

Weekend one was 21 to 25 August for pre-orders. The open beta runs 28 August to 1 September on all platforms, Switch 2 included.
