MW4 Pro Settings

Beta class setups with every attachment, plus the controller settings underneath. Pros carried their Black Ops 7 numbers straight over — same sens, same FOV.

Nobody rebuilt their setup for the beta. Most of the league has been on the same sensitivity for years, so a new game with new maps and new movement is the last place they'd want to relearn their stick. They carried everything over and started playing.

Which means the settings already on this site are what pros are using in MW4 right now. The classes are the part that is genuinely new, so those are captured build by build as players show them on stream. If anyone changes anything, it gets updated here.

Likely to change

The classes and attachments

The game is still in beta and nobody has had long with it. Pros have not worked through every barrel, grip and ammunition type on every gun yet — the builds above are what feels good to them right now, put together in a handful of days.

Expect them to move. They will change as players get a proper feel for how each gun handles, as the meta settles, and as the devs push balance updates between now and launch. Read a beta class as a starting point, not a finished answer.

Unlikely to change

Sens, deadzones and layout

These are muscle memory. Pros have spent years building aim around a specific sensitivity and a specific stick range, and they would rather keep it than rebuild it for a new title. That is why the settings already on this site work for MW4 — they came over from Black Ops 7 mostly untouched.

Changes here are rare, and when they happen they are usually small. Anyone who does move a number gets updated here.

Beta values are a snapshot. Menus change between weekends and change again at launch. Everything above came off a live stream on 25 Aug 2026 and follows the same verification standard as the rest of the database.
