Black Ops 7's competitive life ended at the Esports World Cup in Paris. Every sensitivity, deadzone and class on this site is now a record of a season that is finished rather than a guide to what pros are running this week, and the 2027 Call of Duty League season will be played on Modern Warfare 4.

The beta is the first point at which any of that can be rebuilt. Pros are in it from the opening weekend, on stream, opening settings menus in front of a few thousand people — which is how most of the Black Ops 7 database got captured in the first place. What follows is what is actually known so far, and what is deliberately not being published yet.

The beta schedule

  • Weekend One — early access. 21 to 25 August 2026, for pre-orders and redeemed beta codes, on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC.
  • Weekend Two — open beta. 28 August to 1 September 2026. No pre-order required, and Nintendo Switch 2 is included for the first time.
  • Full release. 23 October 2026. Digital pre-orders get campaign access from 16 October.

Progression carries between the two weekends, and the level cap rises from 20 to 30 for Weekend Two, which unlocks the rest of the beta's weapons, perks and equipment. Warzone joins in Weekend Two with Resurgence on a new map.

Why the numbers on this site will not just carry over

A sensitivity value is not portable between Call of Duty titles, and it is less portable than most people assume. The number only means something against that game's turn rate, stick scaling and aim response curve, and those are set by the studio. Black Ops 7 was Treyarch. Modern Warfare 4 is Infinity Ward. The four fields worth watching:

Deadzones. Black Ops 7 expresses stick minimum and maximum as whole numbers — the 3 / 99 right stick most of the league runs sits on that scale, as does the 75 left stick maximum used to hit full movement speed sooner. Infinity Ward's recent titles have used decimals for the same two fields. If MW4 keeps that convention, there is no clean conversion, and a straight copy of a BO7 deadzone into MW4 is a guess wearing a number.

Movement. MW4 ships with a reworked movement system and a dedicated Mobility Course built to teach it. Movement drives sensitivity: how fast a player needs to turn depends on how they get around a map, which is why sensitivities shifted across the league when omnimovement arrived.

Aim response curve. Curve names have been shared between titles before while behaving differently underneath. Until someone puts an MW4 curve next to a BO7 one on the same stick, the label is not evidence.

ADS multipliers and classes. Per-zoom ADS multipliers only mean something against the optics that exist, and MW4 rebuilds Gunsmith with a new attachment tier unlocked by levelling a weapon. The competitive ruleset for 2027 does not exist yet either, so the classes section stays empty until the league publishes a GA list rather than being filled with beta loadouts.

Field of view is the exception. It is the one setting that tends to survive a title change intact, because it is a preference about how much of the screen you want rather than a tuning value. The tracked BO7 league average is 101.2, and most pros sit between 100 and 120 and stay there for years.

What to run in the beta in the meantime

Not pro MW4 settings — those do not exist yet. But if you are going into the open beta and want a starting point rather than the defaults, the Black Ops 7 league data still tells you where competitive players tend to land, and those tendencies survive better than the exact figures:

  • FOV wide, then leave it. The tracked league sits at 101.2 on average. Going wide costs you target size at range and buys you awareness; pros pick a number early and stop touching it.
  • Right stick minimum low, not zero. 3 is the effective league floor on BO7 rather than 0, because a floor of zero picks up stick drift on a worn pad.
  • Left stick maximum below the top of the range. The league runs 75 to reach full movement speed with less stick travel. Whatever MW4's scale turns out to be, that intent transfers.
  • Sensitivity: start slower than you think. The tracked BO7 average is 1.74, which is lower than the numbers most ranked players quote. New movement system, new maps — a slower stick is easier to raise later than a fast one is to unlearn.

The reasoning behind all four is in the guides, and the full BO7 distributions are on the league stats page.

What gets published here, and when

The same fields tracked for Black Ops 7 — sensitivity, ADS multipliers by zoom, deadzones, response curve, FOV, button layout, grip, controller and board, graphics and audio, and competitive classes — carry straight across to MW4. The schema does not change. Only the values do.

Beta captures go up first and are labelled as beta values, because a beta menu is a snapshot: settings change between weekends and change again at launch. Season data starts when the 2027 rosters are locked and pros are practising on the retail build. Everything follows the same verification standard as the existing database — a source and a capture date, or the field stays blank.

In the meantime the Black Ops 7 database stays exactly where it is. All 51 players, 12 team pages, the stats and the research articles remain up and labelled as BO7. Nothing is being overwritten to make room.

Frequently asked

What sensitivity do CDL pros use in Modern Warfare 4?

Nothing is confirmed yet. The MW4 beta only opened on 21 August 2026 and no professional match has been played on the game, so any site publishing MW4 pro sensitivities today is either copying Black Ops 7 numbers across or guessing. Captured beta settings will be posted here as they appear on stream, labelled as beta values.

Do Black Ops 7 settings transfer to Modern Warfare 4?

Not directly. Field of view usually survives a title change intact, and stick layout and grip are habits rather than settings. Sensitivity, deadzones and aim response curve are tied to each game's own turn rate and stick scaling, and Treyarch and Infinity Ward have not historically used the same scales for them.

When does the Modern Warfare 4 beta run?

Weekend One ran 21 to 25 August 2026 as early access for pre-orders and beta codes. Weekend Two is the open beta, 28 August to 1 September 2026, with no pre-order needed and Nintendo Switch 2 included.

When does Modern Warfare 4 come out?

23 October 2026, on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2. Digital pre-orders get campaign access from 16 October.

Is the Black Ops 7 database staying up?

Yes. All 51 tracked players, 12 team pages and the league stats stay exactly as they are. BO7 settings are now a record of a finished competitive season rather than a live reference, and they are labelled that way, but nothing is being deleted or overwritten.
