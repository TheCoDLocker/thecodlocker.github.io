Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CODLOCKER is a static informational website. It does not require accounts, does not have login functionality, and does not ask for, collect, or store any personal information from visitors.

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information about visits (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when the site loads. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles those requests.

Advertising and cookies. This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data from sites like this one at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. Users in the EEA/UK may be shown a consent prompt for personalized advertising.

What is never collected. There is no contact form storing submissions, no newsletter list, no account system, no comment section and no analytics package. There is nowhere for a profile of you to be kept. Email sent to the address on the contact page sits in an ordinary inbox and is used only to reply to you.

Cookies. CODLOCKER sets none of its own. The cookies you may encounter here come from Google AdSense and its advertising partners. Which ones, what they do and how to refuse them are set out in full on the cookie policy.

Children. This site is written for a competitive gaming audience and is not directed at children under 13. No age information is requested and none is held.

Your rights. Because no personal data is stored, there is normally nothing here to access, correct or delete. Requests concerning data held by Google through its advertising cookies go to Google directly, since that data never reaches us. If you believe something on this site identifies you personally, write to us and we will look at it.

Player information. The site publishes gameplay-related information about professional Call of Duty League players (in-game settings and equipment) sourced from the players' own public streams, public chat commands, and public broadcasts.

If this policy changes — for example, if analytics are added in the future — this page will be updated to reflect it.
