Answers

Frequently asked

What the numbers above work out to in practice.

What sensitivity does HyDra run?

1.7 on both sticks, which happens to be the busiest number in the league.

Is HyDra's FOV unusual?

No. HyDra runs 100, and 18 of the 45 tracked players use the same number.

Does HyDra play claw?

Yes, one of nine tracked players on it. The index finger covers the face buttons so the thumb never has to leave the stick.

Can you really play on a zero deadzone?

HyDra does, on the left stick, running 0/75 left and 3/99 right. It only holds up on sticks with no drift at rest. The right stick sits at 3, low enough for fine aim control without dipping into stick drift.

What controller does HyDra use?

A PS4 controller, which puts HyDra in the minority. Most of the tracked field is on something custom.
